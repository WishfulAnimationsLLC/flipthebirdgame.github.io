Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 7, 2026

Wishful Animations LLC operates the mobile game "Flip the Bird".

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our Game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, the Game may collect certain non-personal information automatically, including:

  • Device information (such as device model, operating system)
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or IDFA)
  • Usage data (such as gameplay interactions and session length)

2. Advertising

The Game uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob, to display ads.

These services may collect and use data such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • Interaction with ads

This data is used to provide and improve relevant advertisements.

You can learn more about how Google uses data here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases.

All purchases are processed securely through the platform you are using:

  • Apple App Store
  • Google Play Store

We do not collect or store any payment information. All transactions are handled by the platform providers.

4. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Operate and improve the Game
  • Display advertisements
  • Analyze gameplay and performance

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information.

However, third-party services (such as advertising providers) may process data as described in their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
